# Multi-Platform Social Campaign Publisher

Turns one published blog post into a scheduled, multi-platform socialcampaign, the right image variant and caption per platform, published through a reliable adapter layer that survives retries, rate limits, and crashes. Built against a provided fake social platform server; no real social accounts are ever touched.
